Line check 1

Fill level, caps and seals

The camera measures the fill line and checks every cap is present, straight and sealed. Short fills and loose caps are caught before they are packed.

  • Underfills
  • Missing caps

Line check 2

The right label, on straight

Each label is checked for the right product, position and condition. A wrong label is the costly one, because it can mean an undeclared allergen.

38% of Australian food recalls in 2025 were for undeclared allergens, with packaging errors among the main causes. FSANZ

Line check 3

Date and batch codes, read on every bottle

The system reads the printed code and compares it with the product and date set at changeover. A smudged, missing or wrong code turns the light red.

  1. Operator sets product and date at changeover
  2. Sensor sees the bottle and the camera takes a photo
  3. The on-site AI reads the code
  4. Pass, or red light and photo on screen

Line check 4

Foreign objects on the conveyor

The camera over the belt spots anything that should not be there, such as a glove fragment or a loose washer, and stops it reaching a case.

Foreign matter was the most common incident reported by manufacturers in the AFGC recall survey. AFGC

Prepared for Daniel Moretti, Operations Manager, Riverbend Drinks

Every bottle on Line 1 checked for caps, fill, labels and codes.

You told us best-before codes and labels are checked by eye on a sample of each run, and that a wrong code after a flavour change is your biggest worry. This plan starts with a free check on your own bottles, then a four-week trial of one camera station for labels and codes, on equipment we lend you. It clamps onto the conveyor and plugs into a power point, so there is no wiring. If it works, the trial fee comes off the purchase and we add a station for caps and fill.

    07

    What we assumed, and what we will confirm

    Assumptions

    • Bottles pass one at a time, at up to 60 a minute
    • A standard power point within reach of each station. If there is none, your site electrician adds one
    • No connection to your IT network or line controller
    • Washdown covers (IP65) on the cameras near the filler and capper
    • Three juices on Line 1, with changeovers set on the date coder

    To confirm on the site visit

    • Peak line speed and how often flavours change
    • Date coder brand and the printed code format
    • Where the nearest power points are on Line 1
    • Which sample bottles and faults to send for the free check
    • Who in your team signs off the trial targets
